How this was scored

Each control is weighted by severity (critical 10, high 6, medium 3, low 1). Pass earns full points, Partial half, Fail none. Controls that were not tested or do not apply are left out, and coverage shows how much was scored. Module scores are combined with fixed module weights. A known exploited vulnerability caps the score at 49; any failed critical control caps it at 69. Grades: A 90+, B 80+, C 70+, D 60+, F below 60.

Known limits: screenshots are reviewed by a person; weak TLS settings are tested with this server's OpenSSL and confirmed by hand when it cannot test them; vulnerability results come from an imported Greenbone/OpenVAS report.
